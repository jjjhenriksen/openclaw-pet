## OpenClaw creature pet assets

The creature artwork used by OpenClaw Pet is sourced from and adapted from
[OpenClaw](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw), specifically:

- `ui/src/components/lobster-pet-sprites.ts` — lobster variants and passer
  creatures, including crab, snail, rubber duck, and jellyfish
- `ui/src/components/lobster-pet-sprites-wild.ts` — alternate lobster
  geometries
- `ui/src/components/lobster-pet-palettes.ts` — lobster palette definitions
- `ui/src/styles/lobster-pet.css` — pet styling and animation rules
